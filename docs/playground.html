Send signals and requests between real CrossBus peers in this page: MessageChannel pairs, two iframes and a module Worker. Every line in the consoles is something a bus actually did.

Signals are one-way messages to every connected peer. The hub sends with signal(name, payload); the agent listens with on(pattern, fn), where * matches any name.

A request calls a handler on one peer and resolves with its result: request(peerId, handlerName, payload) on the client, handle(name, fn) on the server.
